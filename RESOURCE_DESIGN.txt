  PROPOSED NEW RECIPES (with effects)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. COMBAT DRONE (has effect)
     Inputs:  2 Steel + 1 Electronics + 1 Advanced Circuits
     Era:     Orbital
     Effect:  +30 military power permanently (like producing rifles but much stronger)
     Why:     Gives Advanced Circuits a military use. Late-game power scaling.

  2. SHIELD GENERATOR (has effect)
     Inputs:  3 Advanced Circuits + 2 Fuel Rods + 5 Rare Earth
     Era:     Space Mining
     Effect:  -20% damage taken on next attack (stacks up to 3). Consumed on use.
     Why:     Gives Fuel Rods a purpose. Defensive crafting for harder late-game.

  3. PROPAGANDA SATELLITE (has effect)
     Inputs:  2 Solar Cells + 1 Electronics + 1 Advanced Circuits
     Era:     Orbital
     Effect:  +5% influence gain speed globally for 300 ticks (5 min). Consumed.
     Why:     Gives Solar Cells a real use. Helps with the nerfed influence system.

  4. FUSION CELL (has effect — new material)
     Inputs:  3 Fuel Rods + 2 Advanced Circuits
     Era:     Space Mining
     Effect:  Produces as material "fusion_cell". Required for Dyson Ring era cost.
              Also: 1 Fusion Cell = +500 energy instantly when used.
     Why:     Creates a crafting chain (Uranium → Fuel Rods → Fusion Cell).
              Makes the path to Dyson Ring require actual crafting progression.

  5. NANOBOTS (has effect — new material)
     Inputs:  3 Advanced Circuits + 2 Rare Earth + 1 Water
     Era:     Space Mining
     Effect:  Produces "nanobots". Use: instantly completes the next build in queue.
              OR: repairs morale by +10 (nano-medicine).
     Why:     Gives Water a use. Top-tier consumable that rewards deep crafting.

  6. ROCKET FUEL (utility)
     Inputs:  2 Fuel + 1 Coal
     Era:     Nuclear
     Effect:  Produces "rocket_fuel" (5 units). Used for Mars transfers and
              future space missions. Cheaper than raw fuel for transfers.
     Why:     Gives the unused Fuel material a crafting path. Makes Mars
              transfers cost a craftable instead of hard-to-get raw fuel.

  PROPOSED NEW USES FOR EXISTING CRAFTABLES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Solar Cells (currently no effect):
    → Required for building Solar Panels (1 per panel, currently free)
    → Used in Propaganda Satellite recipe
    → Can be "installed" for permanent +2% solar panel efficiency

  Fuel Rods (currently no effect):
    → Required for Nuclear Power Plants (2 per plant, currently free)
    → Used in Shield Generator recipe
    → Used in Fusion Cell recipe (chain to Dyson Ring)

  Advanced Circuits (currently no effect):
    → Required for era advancement to Space Mining (add to ERA_COSTS)
    → Used in Combat Drone, Shield Generator, Propaganda Satellite, Fusion Cell
    → Required for Mars Relay Antenna (currently just electronics)

  Water (currently no source, no use):
    → Produced by transferring Martian Ice to Earth
    → Used in Nanobots recipe
    → Can be consumed for +5 morale ("public water supply")
    → Required for Greenhouse rooms on Mars (future)

  Fuel (currently hard to get):
    → Produced by transferring CO2 from Mars
    → Used in Rocket Fuel recipe
    → Required for Mars base upgrades (already works)

  CRAFTING CHAIN VISUALIZATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  TIER 1 (Fossil):
    Iron + Coal → Steel

  TIER 2 (Nuclear):
    Copper + Silicon → Electronics
    Uranium + Steel → Fuel Rods
    Fuel + Coal → Rocket Fuel (NEW)

  TIER 3 (Solar/Orbital):
    Silicon + Copper → Solar Cells
    Rare Earth + Electronics → Advanced Circuits
    Steel + Electronics + Adv. Circuits → Combat Drone (NEW, gives power)
    Solar Cells + Electronics + Adv. Circuits → Propaganda Satellite (NEW, influence boost)

  TIER 4 (Space Mining):
    Fuel Rods + Adv. Circuits → Fusion Cell (NEW, energy/era requirement)
    Adv. Circuits + Fuel Rods + Rare Earth → Shield Generator (NEW, defense)
    Adv. Circuits + Rare Earth + Water → Nanobots (NEW, instant build/heal)

  3. Propaganda Sat     → +5% influence speed for 5 min (consumable)

  All 5 have clear, impactful effects that make crafting feel meaningful
  and create real progression through the tech tiers.
